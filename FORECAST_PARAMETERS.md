# Tornado Forecast Parameter Guide

| Parameter | Meaning | Caveat |
|---|---|---|
| MLCAPE (J/kg) | Parcel buoyancy from mixed layer | Very high CAPE alone does not mean tornadoes |
| MLCIN (J/kg) | Inhibition suppressing initiation | A cap can favor isolated storms until it breaks |
| 0–6 km bulk shear (kt) | Deep-layer storm organization | Shear direction and storm mode matter |
| 0–1 km SRH (m²/s²) | Low-level streamwise vorticity relative to storm | Depends on storm motion; near-boundary values vary |
| 0–500 m SRH (m²/s²) | Very-low-level streamwise vorticity | Sensitive to surface winds, sampling and storm motion |
| LCL (m AGL) | Approximate lifted condensation level | Low bases alone cannot predict tornadoes |
| 0–3 km CAPE (J/kg) | Low-level buoyancy | Often relevant to stretching and updraft acceleration |
| Effective STP | Composite favorability for significant tornado environments | Not a tornado probability or EF rating |
| Effective inflow layer | Parcel layer supplying storm updraft | Elevated storms may not ingest surface vorticity |
| 700–500 mb lapse rate (°C/km) | Midlevel cooling and instability | Must consider moisture and shear jointly |
| Surface theta-e / moisture convergence | Air mass and boundary structure | Boundaries can move rapidly |
| Reflectivity / SRV / CC | Storm precipitation, velocity and debris clues | SRV is not rotation by itself; CC debris signature needs corroboration |

Prioritize trends, data validity, uncertainty and storm mode. Correlate analyses with actual observed tornado outcomes using archived data.
