Forecast reasoning workflow

StepQuestions to ask
1. InitiationWill storms form? Where will CIN erode and forcing intersect moisture?
2. Storm modeWill storms remain discrete or merge into a line? Is inflow surface-based?
3. Low-level environmentWhere are backed surface winds, low LCLs, low-level CAPE, and SRH maximized?
4. Boundary interactionAre warm fronts, drylines, or outflow boundaries locally enhancing vorticity?
5. VerificationHow does this forecast compare with subsequent radar and official reports?
